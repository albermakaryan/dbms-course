Left rows A, B, C; right rows B, C, D. B and C match. INNER keeps only the pairs; LEFT adds A with NULLs; RIGHT adds D; FULL adds both. CROSS JOIN is the odd one out: every combination, no matching at all. RIGHT JOIN: orders RIGHT JOIN customers = customers LEFT JOIN orders (602 either way). This course writes LEFT always, as a style choice. FULL JOIN reconciles two lists: customers vs employees by name: 28 customer only, 6 employee only, 2 on both lists. Hold on to those 2 for Part 5. Payoff to demo: rebuild sales from orders JOIN customers JOIN products LEFT JOIN employees, EXCEPT SELECT * FROM sales: 0 rows both ways. The split was lossless.
