Source: E. F. Codd, “A Relational Model of Data for Large Shared Data Banks”, CACM 13(6):377–387, 1970; rows from orders and customers

E. F. Codd, 1970. The key idea for the whole lecture: relationships are stored as values, not pointers. The dashed lines aren't stored anywhere; the database finds them when you ask, by matching equal values. You describe the result; the database works out how to get it. That's the declarative idea from Lecture 2.
