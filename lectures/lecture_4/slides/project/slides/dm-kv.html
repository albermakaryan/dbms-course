Key-value: give a key, get a value

One question, answered extremely fast: what’s under this key? Anything else, like “which carts hold AirPods?”, means reading every value.

Source: AWS, “What is a key-value database?” (sessions, carts, caching). The keys and values drawn here are illustrations, not our data
