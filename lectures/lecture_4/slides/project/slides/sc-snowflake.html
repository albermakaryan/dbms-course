Shape 4: the snowflake schema

normalized again: category and branch move into their own tables.

Less repetition, one more join per question.

The Kimball Group advises against it: harder for business users to navigate, can slow queries, and holds nothing the flat dimension doesn’t.

Sources: Kimball Group, “Snowflaked Dimensions”; Wikipedia, “Snowflake schema”

Kimball Group, Snowflaked Dimensions: normalizing a dimension's hierarchy creates a snowflake; they advise against it because snowflakes are hard for business users to understand and navigate and can hurt query performance, while a flat dimension holds the same information. Wikipedia, Snowflake schema: the space saved is often negligible compared with the added complexity.
