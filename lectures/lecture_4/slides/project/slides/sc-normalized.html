Shape 1: normalized (3NF)

one fact, one place. Each customer, product and employee is stored once.

orders holds only keys that point at them.

Every question with a name in it needs a join.

Sources: Lecture 2 notes, Part 2 (normal forms); lecture_4/steps/00-setup.sql (tables and keys)

What we have: customers, employees, products, orders. Lecture 2 built it so an update, insert or delete can't leave the data contradicting itself. employees.managerID is new this lecture and points back into employees (the 'manages' loop). employeeID on orders is optional: NULL for online orders. Cost: every question with a name, category or branch in it needs a join.
